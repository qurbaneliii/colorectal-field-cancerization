# Scientific limitations

Public study counts are not eligible independent endpoint counts. Multiple
biopsies, anatomical variation, near-lesion effects, selection into the study,
clinical covariates and assay differences can each affect interpretation.
Acquisition matching improves the comparison but cannot establish a causal cancer
field effect. Histology, treatment and tumor distance must be verified per sample.

GSE44076 procurement remains aliased with cohort. The previously exposed GSE4107
and GSE41258 evaluations cannot become untouched validation. Controlled JGA
access has not been obtained. No clinical utility, early-detection benefit,
prospective diagnostic performance or independent human approval is claimed.
Historical TAR-byte identity remains unresolved under Issue #5.
