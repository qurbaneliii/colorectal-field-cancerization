# Public dataset research

Public sources were reassessed on 2026-11-17. JGAD000785 remains a controlled-access
candidate. The publication describes endoscopic step biopsies across intestinal
sites and includes healthy participants and untreated CRC cases. Near-lesion
normal biopsies require separate analysis. A full donor/sample/assay dictionary
has not been obtained. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-024-02159-9)

The machine-readable source register lists newly reassessed alternatives and
exclusion reasons. GSE73487 concerns microRNA, GSE199057 DNA methylation, and
GSE176499 healthy single-cell samples. GSE8671 concerns adenomas. None supplies
the required matched bulk-transcriptomic active-CRC/healthy comparison.
E-MTAB-12022 studies liver metastases and liver tissue, not normal colonic mucosa.
These findings do not prove that no eligible public cohort exists. The previous
Phase II registry, including GSE23194/GSE23011 and their overlap concern, remains
applicable. [Evidence register](../../../results/phase_iii/independent_validation/sources.csv)

No new cohort was scored, no restricted data were downloaded and no application
was submitted. GEO/SRA, BioStudies/ArrayExpress and publications were searched;
the public JGA accession is not equivalent to unrestricted FASTQ availability.
