# Access and governance requirements

Before submission, an authorized investigator must provide the institution,
research purpose, dataset request, named users, research period, ethics approval
or documented exemption, and required institutional signoff. A DDBJ account and
the official application process are required. No credentials or identities are
invented by this project. [Data-use process](https://humandbs-production.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/en/data-use)

The responsible PI must confirm compliance with the applicable Type I security
checklist, including restricted accounts, approved storage, user records, audit
readiness, incident response and deletion/retention procedures. Annual reporting
and any off-premise responsibility agreement must be checked against the approval.
The current OneDrive workspace and public Actions runners are not approved for
these controlled records. [Security requirements](https://humandbs-production.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/en/guidelines/security-guidelines-for-users)

Request draft purpose: assess a locked Task B score in acquisition-matched,
histologically normal mucosa with donor-level uncertainty and site/distance
stratification. Request the minimal RNA-seq and donor/sample/assay metadata needed
for this question. Required investigator, institution, ethics and compute fields
remain blank until the authorized owner supplies verified information.

Only public metadata, protocols, synthetic fixtures and approved aggregate
results may enter GitHub. Approval documents, donor crosswalks, FASTQ, expression,
individual predictions and small-cell summaries stay in the approved environment.
The CLI's approval-record check validates supplied fields; it does not confer
permission or independently verify legal authorization.
