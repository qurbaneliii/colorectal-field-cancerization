# JGAD000785 eligibility

The public dataset lists RNA-seq for 78 donors, comprising 17 healthy, six adenoma,
41 early-CRC and 14 advanced-CRC participants. There are repeated normal-mucosa
specimens per donor. RNA-seq uses stranded mRNA libraries and paired 100-bp
NovaSeq reads. The 2.2-TB total also includes exome data; WES is unnecessary for
this proposed expression validation. [Dataset](https://humandbs-production.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/en/dataset/JGAD000785)

Proposed primary eligibility is one histologically confirmed normal sigmoid
biopsy per adult donor, obtained endoscopically before treatment. Healthy donors
must have no concurrent adenoma/CRC. CRC biopsies must have a documented distance
of at least 10 cm from the lesion. This distance is a prespecified research design
choice, not a known property of every deposited specimen. Unknown histology,
distance, QC, permissions or overlap status excludes a specimen pending review.
The earliest source-record biopsy order selects among otherwise eligible samples.
Selection must precede expression/outcome performance inspection.

Near-lesion 1–2-cm biopsies, ileum, other sites and adenoma participants are excluded
from the primary comparison. They may support prespecified secondary strata.
If the primary site/distance yields insufficient donors, report infeasibility;
amend the protocol prospectively before examining scores rather than silently
pooling sites. Donor overlap must be checked against earlier publications and
development cohorts, including aliases that string matching cannot discover.

The eligibility validator preserves source tissue and source record values,
records reasons, rejects conflicting donor diagnoses and requires one primary
specimen per donor. Actual eligible counts remain unknown.
