## Baseline decision

The existing project has a scientifically valid foundation and should be
extended in place. The minimum justified revision is limited to: (1) a
training-only two-PC composition sensitivity for Task B, (2) canonical and
per-gene traceability artifacts, (3) a complete biological-versus-predictive
intersection, (4) a detailed generated final report, (5) artifact-provenance and
contract-test hardening, and (6) fresh local and remote verification. No model
family, preprocessing architecture, cohort definition, or primary conclusion
should be replaced without new contrary evidence.
