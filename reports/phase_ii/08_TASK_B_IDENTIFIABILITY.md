# TASK B IDENTIFIABILITY

The Task B design has 98 cancer-associated normal and 50 healthy donors. With C marking case status and A marking surgical acquisition, [1,C,A] contains only [1,1,1] and [1,0,0]. Its rank is 2/3. Opposing changes to the two coefficients preserve every fitted value. Age, sex and site adjustments cannot resolve the alias.

Clinical pathway and recruitment affect procurement. Procurement affects ischemia, tissue handling and cell mixture. Disease and site may also influence cell mixture and expression. Age, sex and coarse site are measured. Processing time, stage, subtype, center, distance and batch are incomplete or group-specific. Paired tumor/adjacent differences estimate a distinct within-patient contrast. Removal of stress genes and composition adjustment cannot recover the missing acquisition cells.
