# EXTERNAL VALIDATION PROTOCOL

Primary question is same-site rectosigmoid endoscopic mucosa in active CRC versus colonoscopy-negative controls. Lock eligibility and choose one specimen per donor before expression scoring. Require histologically normal colorectal mucosa, comparable preparation and acquisition, known donor/site, pre-treatment sampling, and ≥10 cm from tumor for CRC in the distant-mucosa analysis. Analyze 1–2 cm biopsies separately. Exclude adenoma-only donors, tumor, ileum, unknown primary distance, and development overlaps. Audit actual distance and eligibility before fixing the donor set.

Primary biological estimand is a fixed-marker expression contrast conditional on site and procurement. Report donor-level intervals and multiple-testing correction for marker-specific tests. Separately assess frozen prediction only after model, preprocessing, mapping and cutoff are locked on development data. Report ROC-AUC, AP, confusion counts, specificity and sensitivity, calibration and score shift. Use 1,000 conditional donor bootstrap draws. Report inconclusive evidence if support or precision is inadequate.

JGAD000785 requires institutional/data-use approval, named users, eligible compute and storage, a donor–sample–assay dictionary, disclosure review and checksummed inputs. Public study counts are 17 healthy, 6 adenoma, 41 Tis–T1 CRC and 14 T2–4 CRC. Determine exact sites, distance, biopsy identity, treatment, batch and eligibility inside the approved environment. [Source study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-024-02159-9) · [Access procedure](https://www.ddbj.nig.ac.jp/jga/download-e.html)

The prototype requires compatible single-sample features, a numeric JSON model, hashed explicit mapping, and source metadata. It computes fixed scores before reading outcome labels.
