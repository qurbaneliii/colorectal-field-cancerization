## Lineage and boundaries

GEO series matrix headers, clinical supplement and CEL member lists feed `scripts/run_data_audit.py` and `src/data/metadata.py`. `R/01_extract_and_audit_cel.R` checks and extracts archives. `R/02_preprocess_gse44076.R` and `R/03_preprocess_gse41258.R` generate cohort-wise RMA, annotation and QC. `scripts/convert_r_outputs.py` serializes expression for Python. `R/04_differential_expression.R` feeds the covariate, composition, stress and enrichment branches. Python nested CV supplies outer-fold predictions and training-fold hyperparameters to compact panel analysis. Full-development cards feed the separate threshold and transport steps. External metrics and predictions feed the publication tables, figures and manuscript. The deposited-matrix route has its own namespace and estimand.
